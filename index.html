Shorts Autopilot

Personal tool by Paolo Sagliocco

Shorts Autopilot is a private, single-user tool that helps its owner produce short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) for his own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Reads public YouTube data (search results and video statistics) through the YouTube Data API to find popular topics.
  • Prepares a short video with royalty-free stock footage, a synthetic voice-over and captions.
  • Sends each video to the owner on Telegram for manual review.
  • Only after the owner approves it, uploads the video to the owner's own YouTube channel, marked as containing altered or synthetic content.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.
